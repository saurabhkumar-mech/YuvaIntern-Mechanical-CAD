# Universal Joint Assembly | SolidWorks 2026

## 1. Project Summary
The Universal Joint Assembly is a mechanical CAD project developed using SolidWorks 2026. A universal joint is a mechanical coupling designed to transmit rotary motion and torque between two shafts whose axes intersect at an angle. This project demonstrates 3D component modelling, assembly mates, component arrangement, and exploded-view creation.

## 2. Objective
- To understand the design and working principle of a universal joint.
- To develop a complete 3D assembly using SolidWorks.
- To apply appropriate assembly mates for correct component positioning.
- To create an exploded view for better understanding of component arrangement.
- To improve practical mechanical CAD and engineering visualization skills.

## 3. Software Used
- **SolidWorks 2026:** For 3D modelling, mechanical assembly, applying mates, and creating the exploded view.

## 4. Materials Used and Why?
The following are typical materials used in universal joint components. The actual materials depend on the design and application.

- **Alloy Steel – Yokes and Cross:** Provides high strength, toughness, and resistance to torsional loads.
- **Hardened Steel – Bearing Cups and Needles:** Provides wear resistance and supports smooth relative movement.
- **Steel or Alloy Steel – Shafts:** Provides the strength required to transmit torque.

*Note: These are typical engineering material choices, not verified material specifications for the CAD model.*

## 5. Working Principle
A universal joint transmits rotational motion from a driving shaft to a driven shaft positioned at an angle. Its cross-shaped intermediate member connects the two yokes and allows angular movement while transmitting torque. The joint accommodates changes in shaft alignment, although the instantaneous output speed may fluctuate when operating at an angle.

## 6. Applications
